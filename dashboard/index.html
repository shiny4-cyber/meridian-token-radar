What the model knows.

A valuation scenario, not a prediction of launch, price or allocation.

TVL × peer multiple × growth × listing maturity × structural factor
01 / ANCHOR

Observed market cap / TVL

Positive-market-cap token records form the comparison set. Wrappers, bridges, CEXs and versioned products are excluded. Shared CoinGecko IDs (or symbols when absent) count once, selecting the largest-TVL record. The row itself is excluded.

Use a category median with at least 4 independent peers; otherwise a global median. Ratios are clipped to 0.05–5×. With no peers, use a 0.25× conservative prior. Each detail lists the actual peer slugs and counts.

02 / ADJUST

Growth and listing history

Growth factor = 1 + clamp(7-day growth, −50, 100) / 400. Missing growth is neutral. Maturity = 0.65 + 0.35 × √(min(listing age, 1095) / 1095); a missing date uses 0.70.

Listing age is days since DefiLlama tracking began, not operating age or time without a token. TVL is capped at $1T for the model. Raw fields remain visible. Structural-product heuristics apply a 0.50× discount.

03 / QUALIFY

Uncertainty stays visible

Moderate evidence requires category peers, at least 8 samples, known growth and listing date, positive TVL and no structural flag. Everything else is Low. No row is labelled high confidence.

The lower range uses the smaller of peer Q25 / median and 1/2.5 (Low) or 1/1.7 (Moderate); upper uses the larger of Q75 / median and 2.5 or 1.7. These are sensitivity scenarios, not statistical intervals.

04 / LIMIT

What the snapshot cannot say

TVL is deposited capital, not revenue or equity. Market cap reflects circulating token supply; FDV requires total supply, absent here. There is no token price, revenue, unlock schedule, parent mapping, official app URL or campaign proof.

Market-cap −1 is preserved as an unavailable sentinel and excluded from peers. No token recorded is not proof of no token. Positive market cap is only a frozen observation, not verified liquidity. Ranks cover the full universe; gaps in filtered views are intentional.

Farm plans are conditional research checklists based on category and chain. Budgets and durations are planning estimates, never eligibility terms. Do not invent activity for an unconfirmed airdrop; skip inaccessible or unexplained products.

Offline implementation: main.ts writes board.json and the byte-identical dashboard/records.dat JSON. The browser fetches that same-origin static file only. No data or executable code is hidden: model.mjs is the exact build model and is included in the source dossier.
