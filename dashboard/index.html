What the model knows.

A valuation scenario, not a prediction of launch, price or allocation.

TVL × peer multiple × growth × listing maturity × structural factor
01 / ANCHOR

Observed market cap / TVL

Positive-market-cap token records form the comparison set; wrappers, bridges, CEXs and versioned products are excluded. Shared CoinGecko IDs (or symbols when absent) count once, keeping the largest-TVL record, and the row itself is excluded.

A category median needs 4 independent peers, else a global median; ratios clip to 0.05–5×, and no peers means a 0.25× prior. Each detail lists the peer slugs and counts.

02 / ADJUST

Growth and listing history

Growth factor = 1 + clamp(7-day growth, −50, 100) / 400; missing growth is neutral. Maturity = 0.65 + 0.35 × √(min(listing age, 1095) / 1095); a missing date uses 0.70.

Listing age is days since DefiLlama tracking began, not operating age or time without a token. TVL is capped at $1T for the model and raw fields stay visible. Structural heuristics apply a 0.50× discount.

03 / QUALIFY

Uncertainty stays visible

Moderate evidence requires category peers, 8+ samples, known growth and listing date, positive TVL and no structural flag; everything else is Low, and no row is labelled high confidence.

Ranges are sensitivity scenarios, not statistical intervals: the lower bound uses the smaller of peer Q25 / median and 1/2.5 (Low) or 1/1.7 (Moderate), the upper the larger of Q75 / median and 2.5 or 1.7.

04 / LIMIT

What the snapshot cannot say

TVL is deposited capital, not revenue or equity. Market cap reflects circulating supply; FDV needs total supply, absent here. There is no token price, revenue, unlock schedule, parent mapping, official app URL or campaign proof.

Market-cap −1 is an unavailable sentinel excluded from peers. No token recorded is not proof of no token. Ranks cover the whole universe, so gaps in a filtered view are intentional; Δ TVL compares the model rank with a pure TVL rank.

Farm plans are conditional research checklists built from category and chain. Budgets and durations are planning estimates, never eligibility terms. Do not invent activity for an unconfirmed airdrop; skip inaccessible or unexplained products.

Offline: main.ts writes board.json and the byte-identical dashboard/records.dat. The browser fetches only that same-origin static file. model.mjs is the exact build model and view.mjs the exact filter, sort and format code, both included in this source dossier.
